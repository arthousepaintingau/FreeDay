Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 September 2026
Published by Art House Painting Pty Ltd

This policy describes how the FreeDay iOS app handles information, based on the way the app currently works. FreeDay stores job and settings information on your device. It does not operate user accounts, cloud sync, or its own server.

Who we are

FreeDay is published by Art House Painting Pty Ltd. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at contact@arthousepainting.com.au.

What FreeDay stores on your device

FreeDay is a job scheduling and calendar app. Information you enter is stored locally on the device where the app is installed.

Job records may include:

  • project name
  • customer name
  • optional phone number
  • optional address
  • optional notes
  • status
  • start date
  • duration
  • buffer
  • created date
  • completed date
  • a unique identifier (UUID)

The in-app Calendar displays these jobs. It is not Apple Calendar and FreeDay does not request access to your device calendar.

FreeDay also stores the following locally:

  • weekend working-day preferences (whether Saturday and Sunday are treated as working days)
  • the start date of the 30-day initial access period, recorded on first launch

What FreeDay does not do

FreeDay does not:

  • operate user accounts
  • provide login or authentication
  • provide cloud sync
  • operate its own server
  • use analytics
  • use advertising
  • track you across apps or websites
  • use crash reporting services
  • include third-party SDKs

FreeDay does not transmit Job, customer, Calendar, or settings data to Art House Painting Pty Ltd or to any third party.

FreeDay does not sell or share your Job or customer data.

Subscriptions and Apple

If you subscribe to FreeDay Pro, Apple StoreKit communicates with Apple for subscription products, purchases, entitlements, and restore purchases.

Apple handles payment and Apple Account / subscription records. FreeDay does not store payment card details, Apple ID credentials, or payment information.

The app uses Apple’s StoreKit information only to determine whether an active FreeDay Pro subscription exists, so it can provide or lock access accordingly.

Permissions

FreeDay currently requests no special device permissions. It does not ask for access to your photos, camera, microphone, contacts, location, Apple Calendar, or tracking permission.

Local storage, uninstalling, and backups

Because Job, customer, Calendar, and settings information is stored on your device, uninstalling FreeDay may remove that locally stored data with the app.

FreeDay does not currently provide cloud backup or sync. If you want a copy of your information, you are responsible for keeping an appropriate backup of your device using the tools available on your iPhone or iPad.

If access is locked after the 30-day initial access period ends, or after a subscription expires, is cancelled, is revoked, or is otherwise no longer entitled, FreeDay does not intentionally delete Jobs or Calendar data. The lock hides the app behind a subscription screen until access is restored. Data may still be lost if the app is uninstalled or the device storage is otherwise cleared.

Data security and its limitations

FreeDay relies on the iOS app sandbox and your device’s own protections, such as a passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID, if you have those enabled.

Because information is stored locally, Art House Painting Pty Ltd cannot access, recover, or remotely delete your Jobs. Security also depends on how the device is used and protected. If someone can unlock your device, they may be able to open FreeDay and see the information stored in it. We do not claim that local storage is immune from loss, theft, device failure, or unauthorised access to an unlocked device.

Children’s privacy

FreeDay is intended for business and trades use, not for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a FreeDay account or online service, because FreeDay does not operate accounts or its own server.

If a child uses the app on a device, any Job or customer details entered would be stored locally on that device in the same way as other users. This policy does not claim any children’s-privacy certification. If you believe a child has entered personal information into FreeDay and you need help, contact us at the email below.

Changes to this policy

If the way FreeDay handles information changes, we may update this Privacy Policy and revise the effective date. The current version will be the one published at this page.
